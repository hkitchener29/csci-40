Normal Day in Kung Fu Training

A typical day in Kung Fu training involves a structured schedule that balances physical exercise, mental focus, and nutritional intake.

Here's what a normal day might look like:

  • 6:00 - 7:00 AM: Morning training
  • 7:00 - 8:00 AM: Breakfast
  • 9:00 - 11:00 AM: Mid-morning training
  • 11:00 - 12:00 PM: Lunch
  • 2:30 - 5:00 PM: Afternoon training
  • 6:00 - 7:00 PM: Dinner

The long days ensure a productive training experience.
